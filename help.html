UNITY

Monthly RT Treatment Tracker

UNITY records the number of patients treated with each radiotherapy technique, the total sittings and machine down time for every machine and shift, month by month. It produces the monthly and annual RT treatment reports for the Division of Radiation Physics, Regional Cancer Centre, Thiruvananthapuram.

It replaces the “Monthly RT Tx Report” Google Sheets used since 2022. All their data, from 2022 onwards, has been brought into UNITY, and the reports produce the same totals.

Getting started

  1. Open UNITY at rcctvm.github.io/unity. It works on desktop computers and phones.
  2. Enter your access key and click Sign in. Ask the Radiation Physics administrator for the key.
    • Staff key: enter monthly figures, edit entries, view and print reports.
    • Admin key: everything above, plus deleting entries and all Settings.
  3. Tick Remember on this device only on your own computer or phone. On shared computers, leave it unticked and click Sign out when you finish.
The menu at the top takes you to every page. The footer shows the UNITY version and release date.

Staff guide

New entry: monthly figures for one machine and shift

  1. Click New entry.
  2. Choose the Year and Month. The previous month is pre-selected, because reports are usually filed after the month ends.
  3. Choose the Machine, then the Shift (only that machine’s shifts are listed), then the Shift in charge.
  4. Enter the number of patients for each technique. Leave a box empty for zero. Total patients is added up for you as you type.
  5. Enter the Total no. of sittings and the Down time in hours and minutes. Add a remark if needed.
  6. Click Submit entry. You are taken to the Submission status page for that month.
There is one entry per machine × shift × month. If that slot has already been submitted, UNITY shows who submitted it and asks whether to Replace it. Choose Cancel if you are not sure.

The Workload/day number under the form is a reference figure only. It is not saved with the entry.

Submission status

Shows every machine and shift for a month, marked Received or Pending, with a progress bar. Click Pending to open a new entry already filled in with that machine and shift.

Entries and corrections

  • Entries lists all entries for a year. Filter by month or machine.
  • To correct an entry, click its row, change the figures and click Save changes.
  • Export CSV downloads the list for Excel.
  • Only admins can Delete an entry.

Reports and printing

  • Monthly report: patients by technique for every machine and shift (I, II, III), with totals, sittings, workload per day, down time and shift in charge, the same layout as the old monthly sheet. A yellow note warns if some shifts have not submitted yet.
  • Annual report: choose Calendar year (January–December) or Financial year (April–March). It shows technique × month, patients and sittings by machine × month, and the technique × machine/shift summary.
  • Click Print / PDF. In the print window choose your printer, or Save as PDF. Reports print on A4 landscape with a white background.
  • A machine appears in reports only for the periods when it was in use. For example, Halcyon appears from April 2023, and the decommissioned Clinac 600 C appears only in 2022.

Dashboard

Totals for the selected year (patients, sittings, down time, busiest month), with charts of patients per month by machine, sittings per month and patients by technique.

Settings

Everyone can change the Display options. The other sections are visible only with the admin key.

Display and font

Choose Default or Courier New. The choice is saved on this device and browser only, so each person can pick their own. It also applies to this help page.

Data sources and import

These are the old Google Sheets UNITY copies data from: the yearly sheets 2022–2025 and the live Monthly RT Tx Report sheet that the Google Form still writes to.

  • UNITY only reads these sheets and never changes them.
  • Year: for a yearly sheet, its year. Leave it empty for the live sheet, and the year is taken from each submission’s date.
  • Import (one sheet) or Import all active sources. The result shows how many entries were added, updated and unchanged. Importing again is always safe and never creates duplicates.
  • To add a sheet, click + Add source, paste the sheet’s address (URL), enter its year, then click Save sources before importing.

Hourly sync from the Google Form

While staff still use the Google Form, turn hourly sync on. Every hour, UNITY re-reads the sources ticked Hourly sync (normally the live sheet). Turn it off when the Google Form is retired.

Storage (Google Sheets cell limit)

A Google Sheet can hold at most 10,000,000 cells across all its tabs, and empty cells in each tab’s grid count too. The Storage panel shows the % space available, cells used, a per-tab breakdown and an estimate of how many more years of data fit.

  • Green/cyan bar: plenty of space. Amber at 50% used: keep an eye on it. Red at 80% used: plan a new database sheet.
  • Optimise removes unused empty rows and columns at the end of each tab. Your data is not changed.
  • For scale: one year of UNITY data is about 180 rows (15 machine-shifts × 12 months), roughly 5,000 cells. The limit is far away.

Machines, staff and workload

  • Machines: one per line as Name | Shifts | active, or | decommissioned for a machine no longer in use. A decommissioned machine keeps its history in reports but no longer appears in New entry or Submission status. The line order is the display order.
  • Shift in charge list: one name per line, written as (ABBR) Name.
  • Workload per day: Machine | Shift | number, shown as reference figures in reports.

Access keys

The staff and admin keys are kept in the Apps Script project, not in the website: script.google.com → UNITY → Project Settings → Script Properties → UNITY_USER_KEY (staff) and UNITY_ADMIN_KEY (admin). Change a value and click Save script properties; it takes effect immediately. Share the admin key only with administrators.

Data rules

  • One entry per machine × shift × month.
  • Total patients = the sum of the technique counts. Total sittings and down time are entered separately.
  • Imported data: when the old Google Form was submitted more than once for the same slot, the first submission is used, the same as the old sheets’ reports. Later re-submissions are ignored.
  • Corrections made in UNITY win. An entry created or edited in UNITY is never overwritten by an import or the hourly sync.
  • In the 2024 sheet, TBI and TSET shared one column (“TBI/TSET”); those numbers are counted under TSET.
  • Every create, update, delete, import and settings change is recorded in the AuditLog tab of the UNITY sheet.

FAQ and troubleshooting

“Invalid access key”

Check capitals and spaces. If the keys were changed, ask the administrator for the new one.

“Cannot reach the UNITY server”

Check your internet connection and try again. On hospital networks that block Google services, ask IT to allow script.google.com and script.googleusercontent.com.

I submitted the wrong numbers

Open Entries, click the row, correct it and click Save changes. Submitting the Google Form again does not change UNITY, because the first form submission is used.

A report total differs from the old sheet

Check whether someone corrected that entry in UNITY: the Source column in Entries shows unity for entries made or edited in UNITY and legacy for imported ones.

The page looks out of date after an update

Press Ctrl+F5 (Mac: ⌘+Shift+R) to reload.

Technical notes (for administrators)

  • Website: GitHub repository rcctvm/unity (public), published with GitHub Pages. It holds no data and no keys.
  • Server: Google Apps Script project UNITY, with its code in the private repository rcctvm/unity-code.
  • Database: Google Sheet Drive › UNITY › UNITY (tabs Entries, Machines, Staff, Workload, Techniques, Sources, Settings, Tariffs, AuditLog).
  • Automatic sync: every code change pushed to GitHub is deployed to Apps Script automatically, and a health check runs afterwards. Edits made directly in the Apps Script editor are copied back to GitHub every 10 minutes, so the two always match.
